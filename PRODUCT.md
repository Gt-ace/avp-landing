# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

AVP Software serves small teams whose day-to-day operations depend on manual
handoffs, spreadsheets, inbox approvals, and disconnected software. The
primary evaluator is someone responsible for improving how that work gets
done.

## Product Purpose

AVP maps frustrating manual workflows, designs a clearer system around the
team's real working habits, builds the software and integrations, and
continues to run and improve it. Success means less work waiting on people to
copy, forward, reconcile, or remember information.

## Positioning

AVP is a small, hands-on studio that works across interface, product,
automation, and infrastructure. It turns messy operational processes into
clear working systems and delivers useful software in weeks rather than
quarters.

## Operating Context

Typical work includes internal tools, approval flows, expense pipelines,
dashboards, accounting and e-signature integrations, email, webhooks, and
customer-facing products where the fit is right. Projects begin by observing
how a team works today, then proceed through Map, Design, Build, and Run.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro static marketing site with selective client-side interaction.
- The public offer covers workflow mapping, product and interface design,
  software development, integrations, hosting, monitoring, and continued
  automation.
- Delivery claims and demonstrations must remain grounded in the work and
  evidence already represented on the site.

## Brand Commitments

The name is AVP Software. The voice is direct, specific, confident, and
low-hype. The brand should feel like a precise small studio rather than a
generic automation platform. The existing AVP monogram and editorial
monochrome identity remain authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

Project records and media exist in `src/data/projects.ts` and
`public/images/`. The site has no testimonials, customer logos, or verified
performance benchmarks, so future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Begin with the team's real workflow, not a generic requirements template.
- Make complicated operational work legible.
- Ship a useful version early and improve it with the people doing the work.
- Own the system end to end, including what happens after launch.
- Prefer concrete evidence and plain language over technology spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive marketing elements must retain a meaningful static experience,
respect reduced-motion preferences, and avoid making pointer precision
necessary to understand or use the site.
